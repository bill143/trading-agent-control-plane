Multi-Expert AI Trading System — Build Plan

Prepared for Bill Asmar · Nov 5, 2026 · Planning document only. No build work starts until Bill gives the green light.

What we are building

A trading system run by a team of specialist AIs. Each AI is an expert in one trading school (order flow, volume profile, price action, opening-range breakout, and others). A coordinator AI weighs what the experts say and places a trade only when enough proven experts agree. A fixed risk layer that the AI cannot override sits between every decision and the broker.

In one sentence
You feed the system trading knowledge (videos, books, courses, links). It turns that knowledge into testable rules, proves which rules make money on unseen data, and then trades only the proven ones under hard risk limits.
What "100% finished" means
A working Windows application you double-click and see working, not code sitting in a repository. Every phase ends with something you can open, click through and judge yourself. It runs in demo mode on sample data until your accounts are connected.

Design rule: The trading engine runs on one always-on machine (your Windows PC during the build, then a hosted server near Chicago before live money). The Windows app, Mac app and phone app are remote controls that watch and control that engine. The phone never runs the engine.

Key decisions already made

Platforms: Windows desktop app first. Mac once it is proven to work. iPhone and Android app for monitoring, approvals, alerts and the kill switch.
AI role: Fast numeric models make trade decisions. Language-model AIs read material, explain signals and supervise. They are too slow to place orders.
Multiple symbols: One engine per contract, one portfolio risk manager above all of them. Symbols are chosen across asset classes so risk is actually spread, not stacked.
Proof before money: Backtest → walk-forward → locked holdout → 60 trading days of paper → live with approval on 1 contract → fully automatic.

AI Architecture, Integration & Training

Every AI in the system: what each one does, how they connect, how they are managed and trained, and how many to run.

1. How many AIs, and why

The system uses three kinds of AI. Only the first kind ever influences a trade decision in real time.

KindComponentsSpeedJob
Decision AIs
fast numeric models		Expert models (one per school) · Regime detector · CoordinatorMillisecondsRead market data, issue signals, decide trade or no trade
Language AIs
large language models		Knowledge extractor · Signal explainer · AI supervisor · Ask-the-expert chat · Strategy evaluatorSecondsRead material, draft rulebooks, explain decisions, supervise, answer questions
Not AIPortfolio risk manager · Kill switch · Prop-firm rulesMillisecondsHard limits. No AI can change them.
Recommended count for version 1: 10 AI components. 7 core experts + regime detector + coordinator + one language-AI service that plays the five language roles. More experts in V1 adds labeling work and overfitting risk faster than it adds edge. New experts join only after the first set is proven.

2. Expert roster and rollout

Overlap rule: Wyckoff, SMC/ICT and auction/volume profile often describe the same market event. The coordinator groups them as one "market structure" vote so they cannot fake agreement by voting three times.

3. How the AIs integrate

Market data→Feature calculators→Experts (standard signal)→Regime detector→Coordinator→Risk manager (veto)→Symbol engine→Broker
  • One common language. Every expert outputs the same signal format: direction, confidence, setup, entry, stop, target, invalidation, plain-English reasoning, and cited sources. The coordinator can add or remove experts without code changes elsewhere.
  • Coordinator. Collects every expert's signal each bar, weights each by its proven accuracy in the current regime, trades only when the weighted score clears your adjustable threshold and opposing experts are weak. Default answer is "no trade."
  • Language AIs stay out of the order path. They explain, review and supervise. The supervisor reads live state every 1–5 minutes and can shrink quotas, pause a symbol or trigger the kill switch. It cannot loosen any risk setting.
  • Feedback loop. Paper and live results flow into the evaluation lab, which re-weights experts weekly using completed trades only.

4. How the AIs are managed

Per-expert controls
  • On / off switch and paper-only mode
  • Version number, training date, exact source list
  • One-click rollback to any earlier version
  • Confidence calibration chart: when it says 70%, is it right ~70% of the time?
Monitoring
  • Live win rate, profit factor, average win/loss, by regime and instrument
  • Drift alert when performance drops below its tested range
  • Every decision logged, including no-trades, with each vote and weight
  • Language-AI usage cost tracked per month

5. AI Training & Integrations module

A dedicated screen in the application where all training happens. Material moves through a fixed pipeline, and nothing skips a step:

Upload→Process→Extract rules→You review→Approve→Coded test→Auto-label history→Train→Walk-forward→Locked holdout→Paper→Live
  • Upload: MP3/WAV/M4A, MP4/MOV/MKV, YouTube/Vimeo/Loom/course links, PDF (incl. scanned), DOCX, PPTX, EPUB, web pages, chart screenshots, CSV/XLSX trade journals, Pine Script / NinjaScript / Python. Drag-and-drop, bulk, folder and phone upload.
  • Process: transcripts with timestamps and speakers, chart frames captured from video, OCR, searchable chunks with source and page/timestamp.
  • Rulebook: the AI drafts setup, entry, stop, target, invalidation and context rules, each citing page or timestamp. Conflicts between sources are shown side by side. You approve, edit or reject each rule.
  • Labels: each approved rule is coded as a measurable test, run over 3+ years of history to produce win/loss examples. Target: 300+ examples per setup. Rules that cannot be measured are marked "discretionary only."
  • Integrations: the same screen manages connections to Databento, Interactive Brokers, Tradovate/Rithmic, the language-AI provider and phone alerts, with status lights and keys held in the encrypted vault.

6. Approved Strategy Library (multi-strategy)

The AI does not trade one method. It works from a full library of approved strategies (ORB, VWAP reversion, absorption, failed breakout, and every strategy you add). While monitoring and training it considers all active strategies, and the coordinator weights them by what is working in the current regime.

Candidate→Testing→Approved→Active→Retired

Each strategy card shows: owning expert, rules, instruments, sessions, sources, test results, live results, current weight, and status.

7. Adding a strategy from a video or link

Paste a YouTube or other link. The AI watches, transcribes and extracts the strategy's rules, then the system decides whether it earns a place in the library:

GatePass condition
1. MeasurableEvery entry, stop and exit rule can be coded and detected on a chart
2. NewNot a duplicate of an existing strategy (overlap check against the library)
3. Edge on unseen dataWalk-forward and holdout results after real commissions and slippage: 200+ test trades, profit factor above 1.3, max drawdown inside your prop-firm limit
4. Adds valueLow correlation with active strategies, or better results in a regime the library handles poorly
5. Paper proofHolds up in paper trading before it gets live weight

You see a one-page verdict card: Add, Reject (with the reason) or Needs more data. You make the final approval.
